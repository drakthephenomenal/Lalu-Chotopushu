Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the app RadhaNaam SudhaRasam (also known as "Guru Kripahi Kevalam" or "Lalu-Chotopushu"; "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is a naam-jap / prayer-counting application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google (or Zoho), we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider.
  • Jap and usage data — jap counts, targets, streaks, history, reading and listening time, settings and dedications you create in the App, stored so your progress can sync across devices.
  • Approximate/precise location (optional) — only if you turn on the "GPS Location" toggle. We use your coordinates to calculate sunrise, sunset and related prayer times, and save them with your settings. You can turn this off at any time; the App never reads location in the background.
  • Notifications — if you enable reminders, the App uses notification permission and a device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver them.
  • Leaderboard (optional) — if you opt in, your display name and jap totals are visible to other signed-in users. You can opt out in Settings.
  • Feedback you send — messages, and the name/phone/email you choose to include.
  • Google Drive backups (optional) — see below.

Google Drive access

If you tap "Allow Google Drive", the App requests the drive.file permission, which lets it create and update only the backup file the App itself creates in your Drive. It cannot see or open any other file in your Drive. To make daily backups work, a Google refresh token is stored securely on our server (Firebase). We revoke this token when you delete your account, and you can also remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How We Use Your Information

  • To sync and back up your jap counts and settings across devices.
  • To show sun-based times when you enable GPS Location.
  • To send optional reminder notifications.
  • To operate sign-in, leaderboard (if you opt in), and backup/export features you request.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) services and is encrypted in transit. Only you (and the app developer for support and maintenance) can access your account data.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Sign-In, Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions, push messaging), Google Drive (only if you enable it) and, optionally, Zoho Sign-In. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

  • You can use the App without signing in with limited features.
  • You can export your data any time with "Download Backup" in Settings.
  • Delete your account and data: in the App open Settings → Cloud Sync & Backup → Delete My Account & Data. This permanently removes your account, jap history, settings, leaderboard entry, feedback messages and stored Drive token from our servers.
  • If you cannot open the App, request deletion at delete-account.html or email us (below). We complete requests within 30 days.
  • A backup file already saved in your own Google Drive stays in your Drive; you can delete it there.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
