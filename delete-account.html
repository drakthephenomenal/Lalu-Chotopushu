Delete your RadhaNaam SudhaRasam account

App: RadhaNaam SudhaRasam (Guru Kripahi Kevalam) · Developer contact: drakthephenomenal@gmail.com

Option 1 — Inside the app (instant)

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. In Cloud Sync & Backup, tap Delete My Account & Data.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2 — Request by email

Email drakthephenomenal@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record
  • Jap history, counts, settings, dedications and sync data
  • Leaderboard entry and online-presence record
  • Feedback messages you sent
  • Your stored Google Drive permission token (access is revoked)

What is not deleted

A backup JSON file already saved in your own Google Drive stays there; delete it from Drive yourself. Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Anonymous server logs may be kept for up to 30 days for security.
